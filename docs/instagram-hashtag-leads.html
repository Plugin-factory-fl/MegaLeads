Instagram hashtag email leads

Prospect by niche with Instagram hashtag lead extraction. MegaLeadsAI collects profiles from hashtag discovery, runs AI enrichment to find emails and phones in bios and sites, and exports your hashtag email list to Excel.

When to use hashtag mode

Hashtag sourcing fits campaigns tied to a topic—fitness coaches, real estate, SaaS founders, local events—where follower lists are less relevant than “who posts with this tag.” Pair with our Instagram email extractor overview for a full picture.

How hashtag extraction works

  1. Open Instagram hashtag or keyword search in Chrome.
  2. Select Hashtag mode in MegaLeadsAI and enter the tag.
  3. Start the run; the extension gathers profiles into one table.
  4. Review emails surfaced from public bios and links.
  5. Export to .xlsx for your outreach stack.

Hashtag leads vs. follower emails

Follower email export anchors on one account’s audience. Hashtag mode anchors on content theme. Many teams use both in the same Chrome extension.
